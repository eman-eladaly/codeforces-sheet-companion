# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 11, 2026**

## Codeforces Sheet Companion

Codeforces Sheet Companion is a browser extension that helps Codeforces users view problem-solving statistics directly on Codeforces group and contest problemset pages.

## Information We Collect

Codeforces Sheet Companion does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information with the developer or any third party.

The extension does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

## Data Used by the Extension

The extension processes information available on Codeforces pages in order to provide its functionality, including:

- Problem information displayed on Codeforces pages.
- Submission information available through Codeforces pages.
- Information needed to determine problem-solving statistics.

This information is processed locally by the extension to provide its intended functionality.

## Data Storage

The extension may use browser storage for necessary extension settings or temporary data required for its functionality.

The extension does not maintain an external database containing users' personal information.

## Third-Party Services

Codeforces Sheet Companion does not use external analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

The extension interacts with Codeforces pages and services only when necessary to provide its intended functionality.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to ensure that the extension processes data only for its intended functionality.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the extension's functionality changes. Any updated version will be published on this page.
